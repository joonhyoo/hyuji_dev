# Privacy Policy

## Scope

This policy applies to hyuji.dev and all of its subdomains.

## Information collected

hyuji.dev itself is a static site. It does not run a backend and does not collect, store, or process personal information. Some projects hosted on subdomains are actual apps with their own data practices — those are described in their own section below.

## Cookies and analytics

hyuji.dev sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking scripts.

## Third-party services

Links on this site may take you to third-party services (such as GitHub or LinkedIn), which are governed by their own privacy policies.

## Data retention and deletion

hyuji.dev itself collects no personal data, so there is nothing to retain or delete. For a specific project's data, see that project's section below.

## Strength App

Strength App (strength-app.hyuji.dev) is a small training-log tool used by a coach and their athletes. Access is invite-only — there's no public sign-up, and every account is set up individually by the coach/owner.

### What we collect

- Your name and email address
- Workout, exercise, and performance data you or your coach enter (sets, reps, weights, and related notes)

### Why

This information exists to run the coaching relationship: so your coach can build your programs and track your progress, and so you can see your own training history.

### Who sees it

- Your assigned coach can see your training data.
- A small number of service providers run the app: Supabase (authentication and database hosting), Resend (sending login codes by email), and Vercel (application hosting). These providers process data on our behalf and don't use it for their own purposes.

### How long we keep it

We keep your data as long as your account is active. If you'd like your data deleted, email joon@[thisdomain] and we'll remove it.

### Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data, but no online service can guarantee complete security.

### Children

This app doesn't have open registration. Every account is set up directly by the coach, who is responsible for who they add. If you have questions about a minor's account, contact us below.
