I've wanted a personal site for a while, mostly because it's pretty cool.

I just needed something that would show my projects as I build them, links
to source and live demos and a simple blog if I ever remembered to write it.

I already deal with stateful frameworks like Svelte and React, and I knew that
I wanted something simpler to work with when I showcase my work.

There's many choices when it comes to these static site generators, (Jekyll, etc)
but I found out about Astro! It's a pretty cool front-end framework,
and it pieces along nicely with other tools I've been learning to use.
